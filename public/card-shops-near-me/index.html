What makes Pokemon Trainer Center HB different from other card shops

Most card shops in Huntington Beach, Orange County, and the wider Southern California area are general TCG shops — they carry a little bit of everything: Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh, Pokemon, sports cards. Pokemon Trainer Center HB (also known as Trainer Center HB) is different. Every single product on every single shelf is Pokemon.
