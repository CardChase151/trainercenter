Trainer Center HB — Event Vision & Vendor Charter

Pokemon only · Huntington Beach
Vision
We build people. The cards are just the excuse.
The Standard
Give the card that hurts.
A gift only counts when you feel it leave your hand. If it cost you nothing, it wasn't the gift.
What We Are
  • Pokemon only. Not TCG-at-large. Not anime.
  • Family-focused — built for generations in the same room.
  • Safe, educated, fun.
  • Build people, not sales. Find the compliment. Lead with encouragement.
  • Authentic and integral over performance and flash.
How People Should Feel
  • Welcomed — not just checked in.
  • Encouraged, accepted, loved on.
  • Energy. A smile. Fun. Not business, money, sales.
  • Like someone actually saw them today.
  • Like they want to come back — and bring someone.
The Wave
Culture here is caught, not taught. Not a man-made wave on a schedule at Roaring Rapids — a real one, formed out at the beach from something you can't see. Be present because that's who you are, because you want to be like that — and let someone else catch it.

The Recognition

We don't recruit generosity. We recognize it. Listen for the story, not the slogan.
1What card did you trade or give away at a loss, with no benefit to you — and when did you last do it?
2What person (kid or adult) did you encourage recently, and how did you do it?
3What person (kid or adult) did you educate — and what did you teach them?
4Why would someone want you as the vendor — not your cards, you?
